Privacy policy

Your journal is yours

Last updated 10.10.2026

The short version

  • We store your journal so it can sync between your devices. We never sell it and never show ads.
  • Private journal entries are locked on your iPhone. We cannot read them.
  • AI features use only your regular journal, never Private entries, and only after you allow them.
  • You can export everything or delete your account from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, or your Apple ID sign-in, and the name and profile photo you choose.
  • Journal: your entries, folders, To-Dos, trips, photos and prompts.
  • Location: only when you add a place to an entry, and only with your permission.
  • Private journal: stored only in encrypted form. Only your PIN or recovery key can open it. We can see only how many Private entries and photos you have, how much space they take, and when they were created, changed and deleted.
  • Settings: your time zone, reminder time, theme and Face ID setting; how many AI requests you made today, for the daily limit; and how much space your account uses, for the storage limits.
  • Support: if you write to us, your email address and your message. Contact Support in the app adds only the app and iOS version, never anything from your journal.

You need an account, with an email address or your Apple ID, to use Bunari, because your journal is saved and synced to it. Everything else is up to you.

We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not collect your contacts or advertising ID. This website does not use cookies or trackers either.

AI features

Bunari has three AI features. They are written by OpenAI, through our server.

  • The daily prompt sends only today's date, never your journal.
  • Suggest a mood sends the title and text of the entry you are writing.
  • Weekly Insight sends the date, title, mood and text of your regular entries from that week.

We ask your permission before the first mood suggestion or Weekly Insight, and you can turn these features off at any time in Account › AI. Private entries are never sent. OpenAI does not use this data to train its models. It keeps it for up to 30 days to check for abuse, then deletes it. The prompts, mood suggestions and weekly summaries it writes are saved with your account so you can read them again. AI is never used to make decisions about you.

Services we use

These providers run parts of Bunari. Each one handles your data only on our instructions and under a data processing agreement. Email us if you want to know their names.

  • A database provider: stores your account, journal and photos.
  • A server provider: runs our server, which handles AI requests and account deletion.
  • OpenAI: the AI features above.
  • An email delivery service: sends the emails for signing up, resetting your password and changing your email address.
  • An email host: holds the emails you send us.
  • A website host: hosts this website.
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, and turning a location into a place name when you add a place to an entry.

Where your data is kept

Your account, journal and photos are stored in the EU. Our server and OpenAI are in the United States, and other providers may also handle data outside the EU.

Bunari is run from Kosovo, which the European Commission has not found to give the same protection as the EU. When your data goes to Kosovo, the United States or another country outside the EU, we rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, depending on the provider. Email us for a copy of these safeguards.

Why we use your data, and on what basis

  • To provide the journal you signed up for (contract): your account and sign-in, saving and syncing your entries, photos, folders, To-Dos and trips, export and account deletion, and answering your emails.
  • With your permission (consent): the AI features, photos, camera, location, Face ID and reminders. You can take back any permission at any time in Bunari's Account tab or your iPhone's Settings. This doesn't affect what was done before.
  • To keep Bunari safe (legitimate interests): limiting how many requests and sign-in attempts can be made, and keeping short technical logs, so the service can't be abused. You can object by emailing us.
  • Sensitive information (explicit consent): your journal may include things like your health, beliefs or relationships. We don't ask for this. When you create an account, you agree that Bunari stores what you write to run your journal; it goes to the AI features only after you allow them, and we never use it for anything else.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and journal until you delete them. A deleted entry stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days, then it is removed. When you delete your profile, it is removed from our servers straight away. We keep no backups of your data, so nothing stays behind. Our providers keep short technical logs, such as the time of a request and the address it came from, for up to 7 days to keep the service secure. What OpenAI receives is deleted within 30 days. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer them and follow up.

Export and delete

When you delete an entry, it moves to Recently Deleted for 30 days, where you can restore it. After that, it and its photos are deleted for good.

Export readable journal creates a file of your journal that you can save or share. Private entries are left out. Delete Profile, in Account, permanently removes your account, entries and photos from our servers.

Common questions

I forgot my Private journal PIN

On the locked Private screen, tap Use recovery key and enter the recovery key you saved when you set it up. Without the PIN or the recovery key, Private entries cannot be opened, not even by us.

Do my entries sync between devices?

Yes. Sign in with the same account and your journal syncs. Entries you write offline are saved on the phone and sync when you are back online.

I deleted an entry by mistake

Open Journals and tap Recently Deleted at the bottom. Deleted entries stay there for 30 days: tap one and choose Restore. Deleted Private entries show there once you unlock Private.

How do I export my journal?

Open Account and tap Export readable journal. Bunari creates a file you can save to Files or share. Private entries are not included.

How do I delete my account?

Open Account and tap Delete Profile. This permanently removes your account, entries and photos from our servers, including anything in Recently Deleted.

Does AI read my journal?

Only when you use Suggest a mood or Weekly Insight, and only after you allow it: Bunari asks first. Then the regular entries that feature needs go to OpenAI to write the reply; Private entries are never sent. You can turn it off at any time in Account › AI. The daily prompt never uses your entries.
